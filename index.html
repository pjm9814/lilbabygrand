About

Lil Baby Grand is a pianist, producer, and songwriter, best known for his piano reimaginings of Latin music (most famously, the viral “DtMF - piano version”).

His production and songwriting work includes “MONSTRUO” with Tainy and Feid, “Ballerina” by Rema, Skillibeng, Silent Addy, and Disco Neil, and a trio of credits on Tainy’s Grammy-nominated album DATA.

When he’s not playing the piano, he’s a PhD student at NYU Stern, studying creativity and the music industry. Research →
