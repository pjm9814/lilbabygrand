Lil Baby Grand

Pianist · Producer · Songwriter

Bio

Lil Baby Grand is a pianist, producer, and songwriter. A former classical concert pianist, he went viral for his piano reimaginings of reggaeton hits (most famously, “DtMF - piano version”), catching the attention of artists including Bad Bunny, Maluma, and Jhay Cortez.

His breakthrough as a producer came with three writer/producer credits on Tainy’s DATA, including “mañana” with Young Miko and The Marías. The album earned a Grammy nomination and placed 3rd on Billboard’s 100 Best Albums of 2023. His credits also include “MONSTRUO” (Tainy, Feid) and “Ballerina” (Rema, Skillibeng, Silent Addy, Disco Neil).

His current projects span Latin urban, dancehall, afrobeats, electronic, R&B, classical, and jazz. Across artist, producer, and writer credits, his catalog has drawn roughly 250 million streams, and videos using his music have been viewed more than 7 billion times across TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook.
